## What is Tense? {#what-is-tense} [nav]

**Tense tells us the time of an action.**  
Tense हमें बताता है कि काम:

- अभी हो रहा है (Present)
- पहले हो चुका है (Past)
- आगे होगा (Future)

👉 *Hindi में:*  
**Tense = समय + क्रिया (Verb)**

Example:  
> I eat food. (मैं खाना खाता हूँ)

### ✅ A. Use of Present Simple Tense in spoken English {#present-simple-use} [nav: Present Simple]

**Use:**  
- Daily habits  
- Facts  
- General actions  

**Structure:**  


Subject + Verb (base form)


**Examples:**

| English Sentence | Hindi Meaning |
|-----------------|---------------|
| I wake up early. | मैं जल्दी उठता हूँ |
| She works in an office. | वह ऑफिस में काम करती है |
| We speak English daily. | हम रोज़ English बोलते हैं |

👉 **Spoken Tip:**  
Hindi speakers अक्सर *“I am go”* बोल देते हैं ❌  
Correct: **I go** ✅

---

### ✅ B. Present Continuous Tense [nav: Present Continuous]

**Use:**  
- Action happening now  
- Temporary actions  

**Structure:**  


Subject + am/is/are + Verb + ing


**Examples:**

| English | Hindi |
|-------|-------|
| I am learning English. | मैं English सीख रहा हूँ |
| She is cooking food. | वह खाना बना रही है |
| They are watching TV. | वे TV देख रहे हैं |

👉 *Hindi logic:*  
"रहा / रही / रहे" → **Continuous tense**

---

### ✅ C. Present Perfect Tense

**Use:**  
- Work completed recently  
- Result matters, not time  

**Structure:**  


Subject + has/have + Verb (V3)


**Examples:**

| English | Hindi |
|-------|-------|
| I have finished my work. | मैंने अपना काम पूरा कर लिया है |
| She has gone home. | वह घर जा चुकी है |

👉 **Common Hindi mistake:**  
❌ I have ate food  
✅ I have **eaten** food

---

## 3️⃣ Past Tense [nav: Past Tense]

### ✅ A. Past Simple Tense

**Use:**  
- Action completed in past  
- Time is known  

**Structure:**  


Subject + Verb (V2)


**Examples:**

| English | Hindi |
|-------|-------|
| I met him yesterday. | मैं उससे कल मिला |
| She bought a phone. | उसने फोन खरीदा |

---

### ✅ B. Past Continuous Tense

**Use:**  
- Action going on in past  

**Structure:**  


was/were + Verb + ing


**Examples:**

| English | Hindi |
|-------|-------|
| I was studying English. | मैं English पढ़ रहा था |
| They were playing outside. | वे बाहर खेल रहे थे |

---

### ✅ C. Past Perfect Tense

**Use:**  
- One action completed before another past action  

**Structure:**  


had + Verb (V3)


**Example:**

> I had eaten food before he came.  
> (उसके आने से पहले मैं खाना खा चुका था)

---

## 4️⃣ Future Tense [nav]

### ✅ A. Future Simple Tense

**Use:**  
- Decisions  
- Promises  

**Structure:**  


will + Verb


**Examples:**

| English | Hindi |
|-------|-------|
| I will help you. | मैं तुम्हारी मदद करूँगा |
| She will call you. | वह तुम्हें फोन करेगी |

---

### ✅ B. Going To (Spoken English Favorite)

**Use:**  
- Already planned actions  

**Structure:**  


am/is/are + going to + Verb


**Examples:**

| English | Hindi |
|-------|-------|
| I am going to learn English. | मैं English सीखने वाला हूँ |
| We are going to travel. | हम घूमने जाने वाले हैं |

---

## 5️⃣ Common Mistakes by Hindi Speakers

❌ I am agree  
✅ I agree  

❌ He is having a car  
✅ He has a car  

❌ I did mistake  
✅ I made a mistake
